I am an Assistant Professor in the [Institute of Machine Learning and Neural Computation](https://www.tugraz.at/institute/iml/) at [Graz University of Technology](https://www.tugraz.at/), Austria.

My research addresses robustness, safety, and resource-efficiency of machine learning. I work on parsimonious and efficient deep learning algorithms that integrate security and privacy considerations to build reliable AI systems. These methods are widely applicable for learning-based computing across various domains, such as computer vision, autonomous systems, and neural engineering.

Before joining TU Graz in a tenure-track position, I was a research group leader at [Montanuniversität Leoben](https://www.unileoben.ac.at), and prior to that I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Institute of Theoretical Computer Science at TU Graz, working with [Robert Legenstein](https://www.tugraz.at/en/institutes/igi/people/prof-legenstein/). I received my Ph.D. degree from [Northeastern University](https://www.northeastern.edu), under supervision of [Deniz Erdoğmuş](https://web.northeastern.edu/deniz/), and my MSc. and BSc. degrees from [Sabancı University](https://www.sabanciuniv.edu/en/), advised by [Müjdat Çetin](http://www.hajim.rochester.edu/ece/people/faculty/cetin_mujdat/).
